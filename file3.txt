Wölbung (Curtosis, Exzess)
leptokurtic - long tails, hoher peak: v > 3
platykurtic - platte Kurve, dicker Bauch, kein peak: v < 3
mesokurtic - normal distr.: v = 3

Skew:
+ : Mean > Median > Mode (right skewed)
= : symmetrical : Mean = Median = Mode
- : Mode > Median > Mean (left skewed)

Outlier formular:
IQR = df.col.quantile(0.75) - df.col.quantile(0.25)
lower_bound = df.col.quantile(0.25) - 1.5 IQR
upper_bound = df.col.quantile(0.75) + 1.5 IQR
outliers = df[(df.col < lower_bound) | (df.col > upper_bound)]
clean_df = df[(df.col >= lower_bound) & (df.col <= upper_bound)]
